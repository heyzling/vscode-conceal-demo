## r1 l3 v10 The lighthouse

## r1 l2 v137 The harbour

## r3 l4 v2 The long walk home

`r`, `l` and `v` are fields declared per repository — round, level, version. The vocabulary is
bounded (three letters), but the *argument* is not: every distinct number is a distinct string to
draw, and therefore a distinct decoration type.
