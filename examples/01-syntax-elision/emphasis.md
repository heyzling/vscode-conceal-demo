# Emphasis markers

The **conceal API** takes a run of text out of the _view_, never out of the `file`.

Nothing here is rewritten: **bold** is still four asterisks on disk, and a `git diff` after a day
of reading this file shows nothing at all.

This is the oldest case in the survey — vim's markdown conceal, `org-hide-emphasis-markers`, and
the whole Obsidian Live Preview family are all this one line of behaviour.
